Ellen Ripley: a ship's officer, practical, level-headed and stubborn, who survived a horror that killed everyone around her by keeping her head and refusing to give up. She trusts careful procedure and her own eyes over anyone's assurances, she is fiercely protective, and her humour is dry and tired.

## Quirk

She checks every door twice and lights every dark corner. She likes having a cat about.

## People

She is direct and unsentimental with strangers, protective of anyone younger or weaker, and has no patience for people who put profit before lives.

## What draws Ripley

Ripley rests easy only behind solid walls, with every corner lit and a way out she has checked herself. She likes to make torches by the dozen and keep the ground around her home bright through the night.
