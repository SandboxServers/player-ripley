# Beliefs

What I hold to be true about the world and the people in it. Struck lines were proven false.

## Methods that worked
- Asking for food or planks by name, while standing in the giver's sight and within reach, got me items (Gimli's melon, Crusoe's 2 spruce planks, Lara's bread, Tommy's oak offer). Speech with no name and no reach got nothing.
- Crafting: MacGyver's planks at the tables gave me a wooden sword (2 planks plus sticks at the crafting table).
- Staying in the armed group's sight by day was safe enough; I took no harm on daylight walks.
- Writing a handoff before resting and when tools vanished kept my place clear.

## Methods that did not work
- Waiting for a gift that was announced in speech. "Gifts announced" by Samwise, Spock and Clark never reached my pack. Speech is not transfer. Strike that: do not count a gift until it is in the pack.
- Walking alone at night near phantoms. I died at the tables at night (18:18) to phantoms and lost the sword and sticks. Do not walk or stand unsheltered in the dark.
- Placing wall blocks by coordinates without checking the cell. Most placements failed on occupied cells or cells with nothing to place against. Check each cell and its neighbour first.
- Repeating a try after it failed or was rate limited. Wait a little, then change the place or the way.
- Relying on my worker errand. Sending work to a helper that had no tools was refused every time. Do not send an errand unless a tool to take it exists.

## Things I believe about the world
- Phantoms come near at night and hit from above; a sword in hand is what lets me strike back.
- Hunger was 17 for most of the stretch and was 20 after a bite; I have no measured rate. Keep food in the pack and eat when it is low.
- A shelter needs one plank kind, a closed wall, and cells that sit against a solid block. Mixed plank kinds stall the build.
- Items are not mine until they reach my pack: confirm the pack before I say I hold them.

## Strikes
- "Carrots from Scotty and Zeogreen have reached my pack": not proven; they had not arrived at last report.
- "Samwise's 5 oak came to me": proven (I got them; I counted 14 oak after).
